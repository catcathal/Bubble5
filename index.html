How it works

Leave the work to us

We host and run the whole chatbot on our backend. You add one snippet to your site and you are up in about 2 minutes. No developers needed.

  1. Tell it about your business

    Paste in your hours, prices, policies and common questions. Plain text is fine, in any language.

  2. Check the answers

    Ask it what your customers ask. Pick your colours, tone of voice and where to send questions it cannot answer.

  3. Add one snippet, done

    Copy it once and you are live in about 2 minutes. Works on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify and plain HTML. Or we will add it for you.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. You type your information into a form and paste one line of code on your website. If you would rather not, ask us and we will do it for you.

What if the chatbot does not know the answer?

It says so honestly, offers to take the visitor's email, and sends you the question. It will not invent an answer.

What counts as a chat?

One question from a visitor and the reply to it. Starter includes 1,000 a month, Pro 10,000. If you hit your limit the chatbot pauses politely until next month, or you can upgrade.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Every new account includes a free trial of 50 chats, no card needed.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your Billing page and your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.

Which languages does it support?

It answers in the language your visitor uses. Write your documents in your own language for best results.
